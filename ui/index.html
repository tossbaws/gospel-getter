            Release notes

            Welcome to Gospel Getter

            A quiet place to read the Bible on your computer. The King James Version and the World English Bible are built in, so it works offline, with no account.

            Here’s a quick look around, or choose Skip to start reading.

            Read in context

            • The chapter you’re reading sits in the middle, with the chapters before and after it faded on either side.
            • Press ← and → to turn chapters, right across the Bible.
            • Pick a book and chapter at the top, or try Random chapter.
            • Click a verse to see related passages.

            Find and keep

            • Press / or Ctrl+K to search the text, or type a reference like John 3:16 to go straight there.
            • Click a verse, and Shift-click another to select a range. Copy (or Ctrl+C) copies it with its reference, and ★ Bookmark saves it.
            • Selected verses can also be highlighted in yellow, green, blue or pink.
            • To back up or move your bookmarks and highlights, use Export data… and Import data… in the ☰ menu, top right.

            Make it yours

            • Aa, top right, has six themes, four text sizes, three line spacings, the translation and a distraction-free reading mode.
            • Your bookmarks and highlights are in the Library, the bookmark button beside Aa (or press Ctrl+B).
            • Press C to compare the KJV and WEB side by side.
            • Your chapter, bookmarks, highlights and settings are remembered. To see this tour again, choose Show welcome in the ☰ menu.
